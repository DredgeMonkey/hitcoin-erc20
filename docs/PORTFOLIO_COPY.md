## Expanded case study

### Context

The HitCoin project grew from an idea to connect a fictional cryptocurrency-themed world with a small real-world blockchain experiment. The intention was exploratory and creative—not an investment product or a commercial token launch.

### Implementation

The historical Solidity contract defines a self-contained ERC-20 token with a fixed initial supply of one trillion HIT. Standard balance, transfer and allowance methods are available; the constructor assigns the initial supply to its designated recipient. The contract does not implement mining, staking, rewards, ongoing public minting, or administrative controls.

### Evidence and outcome

The original source is available for review in this curated public repository. Its historical compilation configuration is documented, and separate archival records document a March 2025 Ethereum deployment and a November 2026 explorer/wallet review. Specific wallet and transaction identifiers are withheld from the public showcase for privacy. This is evidence of a completed historical implementation, **not** of independent live-chain verification in this release or of a third-party audit.

### Why it matters

HitCoin demonstrates the ability to take a narrowly defined concept into a working standards-based implementation, using Solidity, Ethereum tooling, and technical documentation. It is one example of cross-domain technical breadth alongside larger systems and integration work.
